Why we partnered.

A land deal is decided in two places: the hearing room, where it gets approved, and the field, where it gets built on budget. Most firms are strong at one.

Tillstone’s partners come from the build side, from excavating crews and public bid rooms to a national homebuilder’s land division. The YES Group comes from the approval side, with a planner’s view of how boards decide. Together, one team can speak to both whether a project can be approved and what it will cost to build, and nothing is lost in the handoff between approval and construction.

Who is The YES Group?

The YES Group (Yokley Entitlement Services) is an entitlement and development services firm that answers two questions on every parcel: can it be approved, and what will it cost to build. It is led by two principals. Charlie Yokley brings more than 20 years in planning and land development, across public, consulting, and builder roles.

Their edge is reading what boards have actually done. Charlie co-founded KnowToYes, a tool that reads the public record of council and planning board meetings, so an approval strategy starts from how a board has voted, not only from what the zoning map allows.

Start at the stage you’re in.

Tillstone and The YES Group cover all six. Most projects don’t need every one from day one, so bring us in where you are and add stages as the work moves.

1

Market selection

Where demand, absorption, and land pricing line up, checked before capital is committed.

2

Site identification

Sourcing and screening sites that fit the product you want to build.

3

Due diligence

Acquisition analysis and underwriting that surface problems while you can still walk away.

4

Concept

A concept plan that tests the site against your program before design money is spent.

5

Entitlements

Rezoning, permitting, and approvals, with consensus built among boards, councils, and neighbors.

6

Development

Bidding, value engineering, schedule and budget control, and contractor coordination through delivered lots.

Who this is for.

Developers. You find the deals. We carry them through approvals and construction without you adding a department.

Land owners. You hold the land. We help you learn what it can become before you sell or build.

Lenders. You fund the project. We give you independent eyes on budget, schedule, and approvals risk.

Capital partners. You’re backing a deal. We keep it on schedule and on budget between your reviews.

Civil engineers. You keep the engineering. We manage the approval process and the contractor bids around it.

Investors. You want exposure to land with a clear path. We map the route from raw parcel to approved, delivered lots.
