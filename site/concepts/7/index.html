Process

First walk to final plat.

The same partners from site control to closing, each accountable for the stage they know best.

Stage 1

Site & market

Due diligence, absorption, and product fit, tested before capital is committed.

Stage 2

Entitlement

Rezoning, conditions, and developer agreements, with consensus built among boards and neighbors.

Stage 3

Construction

Bid the work, value-engineer the sitework, and manage contractors against budget and schedule.

Stage 4

Capital & draws

Budgets built to be lent against; draws reviewed, certified, and reported to lenders.

Stage 5

Delivery

Final plat, lots delivered to homebuilders, and closing, with project controls to the end.
