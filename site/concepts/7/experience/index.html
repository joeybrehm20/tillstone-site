Bluejack Falls

Raleigh, NC (Wake County) · Single family · Multifamily · Apartments
1,200Units
300Acres
Read the full record

Situation

Two raw-land parcels in Raleigh. No unified plan, no entitlement.

Services delivered

  • Directed a $70M-plus sitework program from entitlement through construction
  • Secured 18 easement acquisitions and negotiated developer agreements with adjacent owners for scheduling and pricing efficiencies
  • Engineered offsite road improvements to NCDOT standards
  • Guided the site through rezoning and a multiphase entitlement process, with individual construction-document sets per phase
  • Sequenced construction against conditions of approval — including traffic-impact timing and a phased first-lot delivery to a national homebuilder that reduced carry costs
  • Balanced earthwork by importing fill from a nearby project and maximized density in every phase

Outcome

Combined and rezoned as a major subdivision: a 1,200-unit master-planned community across 300 acres.

River Mews Estates

Clayton, NC (Johnston County) · Single family · Townhomes · Multifamily
825Units
275Acres
Read the full record

Situation

275 acres in Clayton, NC — Johnston County’s fastest-growing city — carrying entitlement risk on unannexed county land.

Services delivered

  • Negotiated a complex developer agreement that reduced impact fees through park and greenway dedication
  • Carried the site through a contested conditional rezoning with neighbors, town staff, council, and mayor — securing target density and product mix with minimal developer impact
  • Managed successive major and minor site-plan modifications and product shifts to keep pace with a changing market
  • Balanced cash flow against builder takedown obligations through a just-in-time, sub-phased takedown model
  • Led the full zoning, design, MEP, and engineering effort to adaptively reuse an existing building as a world-class community amenity
  • Resolved unpermitted impacts left by the prior owner with the Army Corps of Engineers — removing existing ponds and completing a full stream restoration

Outcome

PUD approval secured and the land annexed prior to purchase, de-risking entitlement before closing. The master-planned neighborhood will deliver 825 units of single family, townhomes, and multifamily housing.

The Wilds at Cedar Mountain

Asheville, NC (Buncombe County) · Single family · Townhomes · Multifamily
425Units
90Acres
Read the full record

Situation

Steep, elevation-driven mountain terrain in a demanding municipal environment, further complicated by the shifting impacts of Hurricane Helene — developed simultaneously for three distinct end users: a for-sale builder, a multifamily builder-developer, and a build-for-rent operator.

Services delivered

  • Navigated a complex municipality to the developer's desired entitlements
  • Managed the land-development and entitlement needs of all three end users
  • Structured the governing HOA and REOA documents to serve each end user
  • Designed and delivered an amenity package of trails, parks, pools, and pickleball
  • Applied multiple sitework strategies to minimize foundation and retaining-wall costs against the topography — a significant savings to the developer

Outcome

A blend of three distinct product types — 80 single family homes, 133 townhomes, and 212 multifamily units — set across 90 acres in the Asheville market, with pristine walking trails, a clubhouse, and spectacular mountain views.

288Units
93Acres
Read the full record

Situation

A landlocked 93-acre parcel in Fountain Inn, SC — one of Greenville County’s fastest-growing communities — balancing density against a 31-acre nature preserve and water management requirements.

Services delivered

  • Secured rezoning and entitlements for a landlocked site
  • Assembled the primary access road through multiple easements, utility relocations, and agreements with neighboring owners
  • Obtained secondary emergency access via an easement within the adjacent HOA
  • Tailored the site plan to minimize the impact of rock identified on site
  • Carried the project to final plat with an engineering partner before handing off to a regional homebuilder

Outcome

A master-planned single family community incorporating a 31-acre nature preserve, sidewalk and trail system, amenity building with pool, pickleball courts, community garden, and thoughtfully designed water management features.
