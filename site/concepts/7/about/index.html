Our story

Every seat at the development table.

We have worked every side of the development table — excavating crews, public bid rooms, the land development division of a national homebuilder, and communities of our own. Every seat taught the same lesson: the quality of project oversight varies widely, and the gap is expensive.

So we built the firm we felt the market was missing: an owner’s rep that has actually been the owner, and understands why every nuance of a project matters. Tillstone brings principal-level experience to developers, owners, and lenders — because better experience means better value creation on every project we touch.

Leadership

Four partners, one network.

Every partner has originated and executed deals across the Carolinas and beyond.

LW

Landon Walls

Partner, construction management

Landon Walls has spent his career on every side of the contract. Raised in his family’s excavating business, he ran crews, graded sites, and installed utilities before joining Barnhill Contracting — one of North Carolina’s largest contractors — where he estimated, bid, and managed public bid federal, state, NCDOT along with commercial projects of large scale and complexity. In 2015 he crossed to the owner’s side at Lennar, rising to Director of Land Development for the Raleigh division: 10,000+ homesites delivered, individual projects exceeding 1,000 units and $100 million+, and a team that delivered 2,500+ homes in 2023 alone.

At Tillstone, Landon leads construction management — delivering clients contractor’s pricing, a builder’s pace, and an owner’s accountability.

DP

Daniel Perkins

Partner, development services

Daniel Perkins has spent 20 years as a private developer — acquiring, entitling, and building master-planned communities and delivering entitled & finished lots to national and regional homebuilders. He has entitled more than 3,000 units on projects ranging from 90 to 300 acres. Additionally, as an owner and developer, he has also delivered more than 1,000 multifamily and build-to-rent units. Known for building consensus among planning boards, councils, and community stakeholders, he brings a designer’s eye and an approvals record to every land plan.

At Tillstone, Daniel leads development services: the full arc of a project, from site control through delivered lots.

JB

Joey Brehm

Partner, land & market strategy

Joey Brehm has spent more than a decade as a private developer in land markets, closing over $70 million in land acquisitions and guiding site selection, rezoning, and entitlement across thousands of units. His network and submarket knowledge determine where projects begin: identifying the site, running due diligence, testing absorption and product fit, and confirming the market before capital is committed.

At Tillstone, Joey leads land and market strategy: acquisition due diligence, market and absorption analysis, and land positioning for developers and capital partners.

DL

Drew Levin

Partner, underwriting & lender services

Drew Levin has spent 20 years as a private developer, capitalizing more than $500 million in debt and equity for real estate acquisitions and development. He has structured and closed financing across the full spectrum of capital sources — HUD, Fannie Mae, traditional banks, and private lenders — underwriting acquisitions, building development budgets, and managing capital from closing through delivery. That borrower’s-side fluency is the foundation of Tillstone’s work for banks and capital providers: he has built the budgets lenders lend against, and reported against them through every phase of construction.

At Tillstone, Drew leads underwriting and lender services: construction loan monitoring, draw review and certification, budget and schedule validation, and completion-risk assessment.
