Tillstone home page: seven directions

Each concept takes its look from something Tillstone’s audience handles every week, not from a real estate website template. Concepts 6 and 7 follow a clean corporate-consulting layout instead. Same facts, photos, and partners in all seven. These are previews; the live site is unchanged.

  1. 1Plan Set
    The home page as a civil drawing sheet: title block, sheet index, general notes, revision clouds.    Open
  2. 2Draw Request
    The lender’s pay application: schedule of values, project ledger, partner certification and stamp.    Open
  3. 3Locate Marks
    Utility paint on North Carolina red clay: know what’s in the ground before capital goes in.    Open
  4. 4Project Sign
    The entrance sign roster (owner, contractor, builder, lender), with every line held by a partner.    Open
  5. 5Topo Quad
    A topographic map with live contours, a legend of services, and projects plotted where they are.    Open
  6. 6Field & Office
    Clean consulting layout: dark split hero with a grading photo, service panels, and a sticky-photo process.    Open
  7. 7Open Ground
    The lighter take: wide aerial hero, one-sentence track record, expanding service rows, and a project carousel. Built out across all five pages — Home, Services, Experience, About, Contact (with a working form) — using the site's real copy and photos throughout.    Open

Concepts 1–6 are the home page only, desktop and phone. Concept 7 is built out across all five pages, since it's the one picked so far.
