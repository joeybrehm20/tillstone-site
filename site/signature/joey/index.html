Put it in Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above (or select the signature and press Ctrl+C).
  2. In Gmail, click the gear (top right) → See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature → Create new → name it Tillstone.
  4. Click inside the signature box and press Ctrl+V.
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose Tillstone for both new emails and on reply/forward.
  6. Scroll to the bottom → Save Changes. Send yourself a test email.

Gmail mobile apps use their own plain-text signature (Settings → your account → Mobile signature); the desktop signature above is what most recipients see.
