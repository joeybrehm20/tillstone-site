## About (2,000 chars max — this is 1,190)

Tillstone is a partner-led land development and project management firm based in Raleigh, North Carolina.

Our partners have sat in every seat at the development table: running excavating crews, estimating public and private civil work for one of North Carolina's largest contractors, directing land development for a top-3 national homebuilder's Raleigh division, and acquiring, entitling, and building master-planned communities of our own. Every seat taught the same lesson — the quality of project oversight varies widely, and the gap is expensive.

We bring principal-level experience to developers, owners, lenders, and capital partners across four disciplines:

• Construction Management — value engineering, contract bidding, and construction oversight that pay for themselves
• Lender Services & Project Oversight — independent loan monitoring, draw certification, and completion-risk assessment
• Development Management — end-to-end management from the first site walk through delivered lots
• Project Controls — schedule management, budget reconciliation, and cost analysis on every engagement

Track record: $750M+ in land development costs managed, 10,000+ lots delivered, and 3,000+ units entitled across the Carolinas.

When you reach out, you're talking to a principal — not a business development layer.
