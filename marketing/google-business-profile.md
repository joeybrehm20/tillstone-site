## Business description (750 chars max — this is 706)

Tillstone is a partner-led land development and project management firm based in Raleigh, NC, serving clients across the U.S. Our partners have sat in every seat at the development table — running civil crews, estimating public and private bid work, directing land development for a top-3 national homebuilder, and building master-planned communities of our own. We provide construction management, lender services and project oversight, development management, and project controls to developers, owners, lenders, and capital partners. Track record: $750M+ in land development costs managed, 10,000+ lots delivered, 3,000+ units entitled. When you reach out, you talk to a principal.
