# Partner email onboarding — Tillstone (Google Workspace, tillstoneland.com)

## Part 1 — Joey: create the accounts (2 minutes each)

1. Go to **admin.google.com** (signed in as joey@tillstoneland.com).
2. **Users** → **Add new user**.
3. Fill in:
   - First / last name
   - Primary email: `drew@tillstoneland.com` (then `dan@`, `landon@`)
   - Password: leave **Automatically generate** on, and keep **Ask for a password change at the next sign-in** checked.
4. **Add new user** → copy the temporary password shown (or use "Email login info" and send it to their personal address).
5. Repeat for the other two.

Each user is another Workspace seat (billed at your per-user rate). If Google asks you to add licenses first: **Billing** → your subscription → adjust the seat count.

Send each partner the temporary password by a *different* channel than the email itself — text message is fine.

Optional, recommended: **Groups** → create `info@tillstoneland.com` as a group with all four partners as members, so a shared address exists for the website contact form and LinkedIn.

>

>
> **1. Sign in**
> Go to **mail.google.com**, click *Use another account* if you're already in a personal Gmail, and sign in with your Tillstone address. I'll text you the temporary password separately. Google will make you choose a new password right away.
>
> **2. Turn on 2-step verification** (Google will prompt you; don't skip it)
> Add your phone number. This is what keeps the account safe if the password ever leaks.
>
> **3. Put it on your phone**
> Open the Gmail app → tap your profile picture (top right) → *Add another account* → *Google* → sign in with your Tillstone address. Both inboxes will show in the same app; swipe between them from the profile picture.
>
> **4. Add your signature**
> Open this link in Chrome on your computer: **https://tillstone.land/signature/[drew]/**
> Click *Copy signature*, then in Gmail: gear (top right) → *See all settings* → *Signature* → *Create new* → name it "Tillstone" → click in the box and paste → under *Signature defaults* pick "Tillstone" for both dropdowns → scroll down → *Save Changes*.
>
> **5. Link the company page on LinkedIn** (2 minutes, big for the firm)
> On your LinkedIn profile → *Experience* → edit your Tillstone role → in the Company box, pick **Tillstone Land Solutions** from the dropdown. That puts your photo on our page and shows the page on your profile. Then follow the page: linkedin.com/company/tillstone-land-solutions
>
> Anything stuck, text me.
